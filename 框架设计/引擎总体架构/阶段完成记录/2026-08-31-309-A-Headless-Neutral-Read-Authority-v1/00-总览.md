## 实现

- `open` canonicalize项目根，验证manifest/projectId，建立process-local opaque handle并执行首次refresh；
- canonical inventory使用稳定双扫描、streaming content hash、portable UTF-8 path和case-fold collision检查；
- `Library/Build/target/.git/.aife/.aife-candidates`、`RuntimeModule/target`及任意Cargo tree的nested `target`被排除；
- symlink/junction/reparse、unsupported entry与between-scan drift fail-closed；mtime或same-byte rewrite不改变revision；
- manifest后续损坏成为新的invalid revision，不回退到旧ready revision，clean refresh不写canonical source；
- bounded snapshot只接受显式canonical source path，返回owned bytes、path digest、revision和opaque root binding；
- 旧candidate digest与change exclusion委托neutral owner，`project-tree-digest.v1`字节协议保持兼容；
- candidate staging/apply/discard职责仍保留在`editor_core`，没有扩大迁移范围。
